What is changing?
Some companies are experimenting with AI permitted coding sessions or repository tasks that test how you inspect, debug, test, and review AI output. This is not universal, and DSA is not disappearing. Prepare a balanced toolkit: fundamentals + practical engineering + communication.

PROJECT STORYTELLING

Project deep dive builder

A project is often your strongest evidence. Build a clear, technically detailed story; expect follow-ups on each claim.

Likely follow-ups to rehearse

  • What alternative did you consider, and why did you reject it?
  • Where can this design fail under load or bad input?
  • How did you validate correctness and measure the result?
  • What would you change with one more week?
